COGS — Cost of Goods Sold
Delivered stock only · landed FIFO cost

Supplier ledger follows each GRN expense’s own billing checkbox: material quantity × supplier rate plus only the selected applicable expenses. Unselected expenses are payable to their own recipients. Vehicle rent, loading, unloading and other costs marked Include in material cost increase the lot’s landed rate either way. Their share enters COGS only when that stock is delivered, whether or not the bill has been paid.
